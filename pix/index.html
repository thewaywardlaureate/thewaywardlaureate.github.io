About UGC NET

The University Grants Commission – National Eligibility Test (UGC NET) is a prestigious national-level exam conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It determines eligibility for:

  • Assistant Professor roles in Indian colleges and universities
  • Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) for advanced research and PhD programs

Held twice a year, the exam includes:

  • Paper I: Teaching & Research Aptitude
  • Paper II: Subject-specific questions

Clearing UGC NET opens doors to academic careers and research opportunities across India.
