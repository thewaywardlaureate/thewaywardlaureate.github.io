About UGC NET

The University Grants Commission – National Eligibility Test (UGC NET), conducted by the NTA, determines eligibility for Assistant Professor roles and the Junior Research Fellowship (JRF). Our programme covers Paper I (Teaching & Research Aptitude) and Paper II (English).

  • Paper I — Teaching & Research Aptitude
  • Paper II — English (subject-specific questions)
  • PYQ discussions, assessments, and doubt clinics
