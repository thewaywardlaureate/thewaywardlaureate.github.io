Delhi Evening Plan

Dilli Haat → Lodhi Art District → Khan Market

🕔 4:40 PM start 🌇 Sunset ~5:25 PM 🚇 Metro + Auto
  1. 1
    5:00 – 6:30 PM

    Dilli Haat INA

    90 minutes to browse the craft stalls properly. The lanterns and lights look best in the evening anyway.

    6:40 – 7:10 PM

    Lodhi Art District murals

    Auto from Dilli Haat (~10 min, ₹60–100). Keep it to 30 minutes and walk a loop around Lodhi Colony Market — the murals are spread across the lanes, not in one spot.

    Open pin in Maps Directions from Dilli Haat
  4. 4
    7:15 – 8:45 PM

    Khan Market

    Auto from Lodhi Colony (~10 min). Walk around before dinner to catch the post-Diwali decorations.

    Open pin in Maps Directions from Lodhi Colony
  5. 5
    7:30 – 8:45 PM · dinner

    Somewhere to eat

    The Big Chill Cafe — share a main and a dessert (~₹500 per head). Ice cream stop #2: split the Mississippi Mud Pie.

    Café Turtle (Full Circle Bookstore) — quieter option, also around ₹500 per head.

    The Big Chill Cafe Café Turtle
Heads up on Lodhi: it gets dark by ~6:40 PM, and the streetlights there are uneven — some murals will be dim. If it feels too dark or you're tired, skip it and go straight to Khan Market.

🚇 Getting back — 9:00 PM

Walk to Khan Market Metro, Violet Line to Central Secretariat, then Yellow Line to Hauz Khas. Check last-train timings on Maps — you should be fine at this hour.
