About Us

PIXELEARN Ed Hub is an academic initiative founded by a group of passionate educators and researchers, with the vision of transforming education into a dynamic, inclusive, and future-ready experience. Designed for learners at every stage, the platform supports preparation for competitive exams, development of real-world skills, exploration of artificial intelligence, and solutions for academic challenges. With expert guidance and innovative tools, Pixelearn empowers progress—every pixel, every step.

Jameel Ahammed CP
